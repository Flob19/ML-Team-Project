\section{Motivation and Problem Statement}
The ability to predict customer demand is a critical advantage for small businesses. In the coffee industry, sales are highly volatile and influenced by external factors like weather. Rain might increase the demand for indoor seating and hot beverages, while sunshine could drive the sale of iced drinks. Our goal was to quantify these relationships and build a tool that helps shop owners plan their operations, staffing, and inventory more efficiently.

\section{Objectives}
The primary objective of this project was to:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Preprocess and merge sales data with weather coordinates from New York.
    \item Train multiple regression models to predict hourly order quantities.
    \item Evaluate the performance of different architectures (Linear vs. Ensemble vs. Neural Networks).
    \item Deploy a user-friendly dashboard for real-time interaction.
\end{itemize}
