\chapter*{Abstract}
\label{sec:abstract}
\vspace*{-10mm}
\\

This project presents an end-to-end machine learning pipeline for predicting hourly coffee shop sales in New York City based on historical transaction data and meteorological conditions. By merging retail data with Open-Meteo weather features, we trained three distinct models: Linear Regression, Random Forest, and a Multilayer Perceptron (MLP) Neural Network. Our results demonstrate that the Random Forest model outperformed other architectures with a peak accuracy of 72.8\%. We implemented a Streamlit-based dashboard to provide real-time forecasting and manual "what-if" scenario analysis, offering café owners a data-driven tool for inventory management and staff optimization.
